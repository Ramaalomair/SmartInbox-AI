# Technical Support and Priority Policy (POL-TECH-01)
Technical Support handles login failures, outages and application errors. HIGH priority for widespread service outages, account compromise, or service blocking many users. MEDIUM for a single-user login issue preventing normal work; LOW for general usage questions. Use lookup_ticket when the message provides a ticket ID. Avoid invented incident statuses, recovery times, or promises. Request diagnostic details if needed, but never request passwords, OTP codes or complete payment-card details.

Ticket notes and resolutions may be disclosed only after verifying ticket ownership. A resolved status is not sufficient to infer the resolution when the resolution field is blank.
