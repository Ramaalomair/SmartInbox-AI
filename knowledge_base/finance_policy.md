# Invoice and Billing Policy (POL-FIN-01)
For invoice/payment status, look up authorized billing records using invoice ID. If no verified invoice record is available, request the invoice ID or escalate to Finance without stating it was paid. Possible duplicate charges should be routed to Finance; if linked to a return or order issue, mention Customer Support as secondary. Never initiate real transfers, refunds, or payments. Financial information is confidential and should not be included in outbound drafts beyond what is necessary.

Invoice status may be disclosed only to approved billing contacts after tool-level authorization checks. An unverified invoice status does not establish a duplicate charge; escalate for investigation without claiming a duplicate payment was proven.
